About

Between electromagnetics and the systems built on top of it

I am a lecturer and researcher at the School of Electrical Engineering, International University, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City, where I serve as Acting Dean and head the Department of Telecommunications.

My doctoral work at the Laboratory of Electronics and Electromagnetism (L2E), Université Pierre et Marie Curie, produced MESUA — a three-dimensional scattering model for characterising how high-frequency waves propagate through dense urban areas, applied to target detection in monostatic and bistatic radar configurations and to the synthesis of SAR data. That thread still runs through my work today, now extended toward VLEO and satellite-based links.

Alongside it, my research has widened into applied machine learning and computer vision — UAV-based remote sensing, indoor positioning, automated visual inspection, and Vietnamese-language NLP — usually built with graduate students on problems that have a concrete instrument or dataset behind them.

A second career runs in parallel. Over roughly a decade of consulting and coaching I have worked on enterprise architecture, IT master planning, and digital transformation for Vietnamese enterprises and provincial government agencies, and I am a TOGAF-certified enterprise architect. Teaching, research, and that practice tend to feed one another.

Research

Areas of work

Five strands, most of which share a common question: how do electromagnetic waves behave in a complicated environment, and what can we recover from what comes back?

EM modelling & radar scattering

Three-dimensional models of high-frequency scattering by urban areas, for monostatic and bistatic radar. Detection and localisation of hidden and moving targets with UWB and Ka-band systems, validated against measurement.

MESUABistatic radarUWBKa-band

Satellite communications

EHF simulation models for low-latency VLEO satellite imagery, and coherent 3-D models for satellite-based VHF links. Space communications as a taught subject and a research line at once.

VLEOEHF / VHFLink modelling

Remote sensing & SAR

Synthesising SAR data from physical propagation models, and using simulated remote-sensing data at scale for urban land-use mapping — bridging electromagnetic theory and Earth-observation datasets.

SAR synthesisLand-use mappingIGARSS

AI, machine learning & vision

Deep learning applied to UAV surveillance, human and object detection, automated fabric-defect classification, face recognition, and Vietnamese text-embedding benchmarks.

Computer visionUAV sensingEdge inferenceNLP

IoT & embedded systems

Instrumented systems that have to work outdoors: IoT and machine-learning monitoring of insects in bio-pesticide crops, ESP32 ultra-wideband indoor positioning, and automated traffic-violation reporting.

IoTUWB positioningESP32

Digital government & EA

Enterprise and business architecture, IT governance and service management, IT capability frameworks, and digital transformation programmes for enterprises and provincial government agencies.

TOGAFITILe-GovernmentIT master plan
